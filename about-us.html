CORPORATE PROFILE & HERITAGE

Rollmann Trading Company

Pioneering precision linear motion, bearing technology, and automated manufacturing systems in India since 1985.

Four Decades of Engineering Excellence in Linear Motion

Rollmann Trading Company was established in 1985 with a clear mission: to bring the world's most advanced linear motion and precision bearing technologies to the rapidly expanding Indian manufacturing ecosystem.

As an authorized stockist and distributor for THK Co., Ltd. (Japan), Oiles Corporation, Zimmer Group, and FAG/INA, Rollmann bridges the gap between global precision manufacturing and local Indian industries by maintaining over 25,000+ line items in immediate stock across 5 nationwide warehousing hubs.

We do not simply trade parts — we provide complete application engineering, customized rail cutting to exact millimeters, precision CNC ball screw journal machining, and 48-hour emergency ball screw re-balling to keep critical factory production lines running with zero unplanned downtime.
