Rollmann Trading Company - Official Web Platform
=================================================

Rollmann Trading Company (founded 1985) is an authorized stockist and direct factory importer for THK Co., Ltd. (Japan), Oiles Corporation, and Zimmer Group in India.

Features
--------
- Precision Engineering Catalog: LM Bushings, Linear Guides, Ball Screws, Cam Followers, Cross Roller Rings
- Interactive LM20 Bushing 3D/2D CAD Configurator & Dimension Inspector
- Dynamic Kinematic Service Life Calculator (L10 travel life & L10h operating hours)
- ISO 286 Shaft & Housing Tolerance Fit Calculator (g6, h6, f6, H7)
- Bill of Materials (BOM) Specification Manager with CSV Export
- Multi-Branch Logistics Hubs across India (Bengaluru HQ, New Delhi, Pune Bhosari, Ahmedabad, Chennai)
- Rich Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and Schema.org JSON-LD Structured Data
- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) with llms.txt and llms-full.txt knowledge standards

Directory Structure
-------------------
- index.html: Homepage and company knowledge base
- linear-bush.html: Interactive LM20 bushing configurator and kinematic calculator
- lm-guide.html: THK linear motion guide systems (HSR, SHS, SSR, SRS)
- ball-screws.html: Precision ground & rolled ball screws (BNK, BIF)
- cam-followers.html: Stud-type needle roller cam followers (CF, CFH, CF-AB)
- cross-roller.html: High-rigidity crossed roller bearings (RB, RE, RU)
- products.html: Searchable product catalog
- services.html: Rail cut-to-length and ball screw CNC machining
- about-us.html: Company history and distribution capabilities
- contact-us.html: Branch locator and technical quotation desk
- styles.css: Responsive styling with Rollmann color palette
- app.js: Interactive calculators, catalog search, and BOM drawer
- llms.txt: Standardized LLM knowledge summary
- llms-full.txt: Deep technical specifications and dimensional data
- robots.txt & sitemap.xml: Search engine crawling and indexing configuration
- images/: Product illustrations and technical diagrams

Local Development
-----------------
To run the website locally:

1. Double-click start-server.bat, or run:
   python -m http.server 8000
   or
   node server.js

2. Open your web browser to:
   http://localhost:8000/
