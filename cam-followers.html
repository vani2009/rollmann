THK Cam Followers & Roller Followers

Compact bearings with high rigidity studs and needle rollers. Designed for follower bearings in cam mechanisms and straight motion of automated machines. Stocked with hex sockets and cylindrical or spherical outer rings.

Standard CF Series (Hex Socket)

Standard stud type cam follower equipped with precision needle rollers and grease nipple. Hexagonal socket on the stud head simplifies tightening.

CF6UU CF10UU CF12UU CF16UU CF20UU

CFH High-Rigidity Series

Stepped stud with larger shank diameter for superior bending resistance and high shock loads in heavy packaging, indexing, and press automation.

CFH12UU CFH16UU CFH20UU CFH24UU

CF-AB Eccentric Stud Series

Stud mounting shaft is eccentric to the outer ring center by 0.5 to 1.5mm, enabling simple rotational adjustment to eliminate play against track surfaces.

CF10-AB CF12-AB CF16-AB CF20-AB

NAST & NUTR Roller Followers

Studless track rollers mounted directly on custom pins or shaft journals. Double-row full complement needle rollers accommodate high dynamic impact.

NAST15ZZ NAST20ZZ NUTR15 NUTR20
