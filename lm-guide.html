THK LM Guides & Precision Linear Rails

The world standard in linear motion. Heavy-load 4-way equal load HSR series, high-speed caged ball SHS, compact SSR, and stainless miniature SRS series. Available in ready stock with custom rail cutting to exact lengths.

Custom Rail Cutting Service

HSR Series (4-Way Equal Load)

Equal load capacity in all 4 directions (radial, reverse-radial, and lateral). High rigidity and self-adjusting capability under heavy machine tool loads.

HSR15A/R HSR20A/R HSR25A/R HSR30A/R HSR35A/R

SHS Series (Caged Ball)

THK patented ball cage eliminates ball-to-ball friction, enabling whisper-quiet motion, high-speed travel up to 5 m/s, and long maintenance-free intervals.

SHS15C/R SHS20C/R SHS25C/R SHS30C/R SHS35C/R

SSR Series (Low Center of Gravity)

Compact design with low sectional height and radial load optimization. Ideal for horizontal linear transfer units and space-constrained automation modules.

SSR15XW SSR20XW SSR25XW SSR30XW SSR35XW

SRS Series (Miniature Stainless)

Ultra-compact 2-row gothic arch groove structure manufactured in corrosion-resistant martensitic stainless steel. Perfect for semiconductor and medical analyzers.

SRS9M SRS12M SRS15M SRS20M
