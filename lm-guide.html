HSR Series (4-Way Equal Load)

Equal load capacity in all 4 directions (radial, reverse-radial, and lateral). High rigidity and self-adjusting capability under heavy machine tool loads.

SHS Series (Caged Ball)

THK patented ball cage eliminates ball-to-ball friction, enabling whisper-quiet motion, high-speed travel up to 5 m/s, and long maintenance-free intervals.

SSR Series (Low Center of Gravity)

Compact design with low sectional height and radial load optimization. Ideal for horizontal linear transfer units and space-constrained automation modules.
