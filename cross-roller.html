THK Precision Cross Roller Rings

Internal cylindrical rollers arranged orthogonally in 90° V-grooves. A single bearing simultaneously absorbs radial, axial, and moment loads with rotational runout accuracy below 2 μm.

RB Series (Separable Outer Ring)

Standard model with separable two-piece outer ring held by retaining bolts. Optimized for extreme rotational accuracy of the inner ring, such as machine tool index tables.

RB5013 RB8016 RB10020 RB15025

RE Series (Separable Inner Ring)

Manufactured with identical outer boundary dimensions as the RB series, but with a separable two-piece inner ring. Designed for outer ring rotational accuracy.

RE5013 RE7013 RE10020 RE14025

RU Series (Integrated Mounting Holes)

Both inner and outer rings are of solid one-piece construction with counterbored mounting holes. Mounts directly to machine frames without dedicated housing flanges.

RU42 RU66 RU85 RU124 RU148

RA Series (Compact Ultra-Thin)

Extremely compact cross-section thickness engineered to minimize robot arm weight and inertia in 6-axis articulated robot wrists and medical scanners.

RA5008 RA6008 RA8008 RA10008
