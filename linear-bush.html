Structure and Operating Features of THK Linear Bushings

The THK Linear Bushing is a high-precision linear motion mechanism used in conjunction with a cylindrical LM shaft to perform infinite straight travel. The balls in the loaded area of the nut are in point contact with the shaft, allowing high-accuracy movement with minimal frictional resistance even under dynamic loads.

Precision Heat-Treated Outer Cylinder

The outer cylinder is manufactured from high-carbon chromium bearing steel (SUJ2 equivalent), thoroughly heat treated to achieve a surface hardness of HRC 58 to 64, then precision ground for extreme dimensional stability.

Seamless Resin Ball Retainer

The one-piece synthetic resin retainer seamlessly guides multiple recirculating rows of bearing balls with low acoustic noise, minimal rolling resistance, and reduced heat generation at high linear speeds (up to 2 m/s).

Shaft Selection & Housing Fit Tolerances

Because the balls of the Linear Bushing run directly on the shaft surface, the hardness, surface finish, and dimensional tolerance of the shaft directly determine the operating life and positioning accuracy of the system.

Shaft Hardness HRC 58 to 64. Hardened depth must be at least 1.5mm to 2.5mm via induction hardening.
Surface Roughness Ra 0.2 μm (or Rmax 0.8S) or finer, centerless precision ground.
Shaft Tolerance Standard: g6. Precision Fit: h6. Loose Fit: f6. Recommended Housing Bore: H7.

Lubrication & Maintenance Protocol

Proper lubrication prevents metal-to-metal contact between the rolling balls and raceways, minimizes frictional wear, dissipates heat, and protects precision surfaces from rust.

  • Initial Grease Fill: THK Linear Bushings are coated with anti-rust oil upon dispatch. Before initial operation, apply high-grade lithium soap-based grease (such as THK AFB-LF or AFA grease) directly to the ball tracks.
  • Relubrication Interval: Under normal room temperature and clean industrial environments, relubricate every 100 km of cumulative travel distance or every 6 months, whichever comes first.
  • High-Speed Applications: In automated pick-and-place systems exceeding 1 m/s velocity, turbine oil ISO VG 32 to 68 is recommended through a felt-ring oiler.

Mounting Precautions & Circlip Assembly

To preserve the high-precision ball tracks and prevent premature brinelling, always observe the following installation precautions:

Mandatory Press-Fitting Rule:

When press-fitting the bushing into the housing bore, always press directly against the outer cylinder end face using a dedicated flat pressing jig or mandrel. NEVER strike the synthetic retainer or the ball recirculating components directly, as even microscopic indentation will cause binding and early fatigue failure.

Retain the bushing securely inside the housing using standard internal retaining rings (circlips conforming to JIS B 2804) fitted into the groove dimensions B and W, or using precision stopper plates.
