Authorized Stockist Knowledge Base & Engineering FAQ

Official THK Japan Verification
Who is the authorized distributor and stockist for THK in India?
Rollmann Trading Company is India's leading authorized stockist and direct factory importer for THK Co., Ltd. (Tokyo, Japan). Established in 1985, Rollmann maintains an active inventory of over 25,000 genuine Japanese line items across five strategic warehouse hubs in Bengaluru, Pune, New Delhi, Ahmedabad, and Chennai.
Where can I buy genuine THK LM guides, linear bushings, and ball screws in India?
Genuine THK LM Guides (HSR, SHS, SSR series), Linear Bushings (LM20UU, LM12, LM16), and precision Ball Screws (BNK, BIF) can be procured directly from Rollmann Trading Company. All components come with factory test certificates, original Japanese packaging, and same-day dispatch from Bengaluru or Pune warehouses.
Does Rollmann provide custom linear rail cutting and CNC end machining?
Yes, Rollmann operates in-house precision machine shops providing burr-free abrasive cutting of THK profile rails to within ±0.5 mm tolerance with custom symmetrical G-dimension end distances, as well as CNC turning, threading, and keyway machining for ball screw support journals (BK/BF, FK/FF).
Which cities in India does Rollmann Trading Company supply to?
Rollmann supplies automation builders and machine tool manufacturers nationwide across India, with dedicated regional sales offices and warehouses in Bengaluru (HQ), Pune (MIDC Bhosari), New Delhi, Ahmedabad, and Chennai, delivering within 24 to 48 hours.
What other linear motion and bearing brands does Rollmann distribute?
In addition to THK Japan, Rollmann is an authorized distributor for Oiles Corporation (self-lubricating 500SP solid bronze bearings and Drymet bushings), Zimmer Group (pneumatic linear guide rail brakes and clamping elements), and precision FAG/INA bearings.
How can I calculate THK bearing service life and get 2D/3D CAD models?
Rollmann provides free online engineering tools at rollmann.in, including interactive 2D DXF and 3D STEP CAD downloads, ISO 286 shaft fit calculators, and THK mathematical L10 kinematic fatigue life equations (L10 = (C / P · fw)³ × 50 km).
