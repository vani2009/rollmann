Precision Value-Added Engineering Services

Rollmann Trading Company provides comprehensive manufacturing, cutting, end-machining, and repair capabilities to support automation builders, machine tool manufacturers, and plant maintenance engineers across India.

SERVICE 01

Precision LM Rail Cutting & Jointing

Equipped with dedicated linear guide cut-off saws, flood cooling, and deburring machines to ensure zero thermal damage to inductive raceways.

  • Length Tolerance: ±0.5 mm or ±0.2 mm on request.
  • Symmetric Hole Pitch: Custom matching of the end hole distance ($G$ dimension).
  • Jointed Rails: Precision ground end faces for continuous travel exceeding 4 meters.
SERVICE 02

CNC Ball Screw End Machining

Custom journal turning, threading, keyway milling, and snap-ring grooving for fixed (BK, FK) and supported (BF, FF) bearing assemblies.

  • Per Drawing or JIS: Standard JIS B 1192 or custom customer CAD drawings.
  • Runout Accuracy: Concentricity within 0.005 mm verified on precision dial indicators.
  • Turnkey Assembly: Delivered pre-assembled with angular bearings and locknuts.
SERVICE 03

Ball Screw Re-Balling & Backlash Repair

Complete refurbishment and precision re-balling of worn ball screws to eliminate axial backlash and restore smooth rolling preload.

  • Ultrasonic Cleaning: Thorough inspection of raceway profile and pitch errors.
  • Micron-Graded Balls: Custom oversized steel balls calibrated in 1 μm increments.
  • Emergency Service: 48-hour plant breakdown turnaround available.
